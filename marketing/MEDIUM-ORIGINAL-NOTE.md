## Why this wording

- **"I have left this post exactly as I wrote it"** — states the choice openly, so the
  reader understands the flaws are preserved deliberately rather than missed.
- **Names the 8.8% up front.** A reader who arrives from search on the old URL gets the most
  important fact immediately instead of reading a superseded article to the end.
- **Names both false claims explicitly**, so nobody discovers them independently and
  concludes you were hiding something. Volunteered errors cost almost nothing; found ones
  are expensive.
- **Ends on the forward link**, which is what turns the old post's existing search traffic
  into readers of the new one. This is why the original is worth keeping rather than
  replacing — you inherit its indexing instead of starting over.
- **Does not apologise.** The tone is a correction notice, not a confession. That is the
  register that reads as authority.

## Placement

Very top, above the existing "Not a medical device" line. Readers arriving from search need
the update before they invest in the article, not after.

## One thing not to do

Do not delete or rewrite the original body. The two posts are only valuable as a pair —
the first establishes what you built and believed, the second establishes that you went
back and checked. Editing the first collapses that into a single revised document and the
arc disappears.
